The idea in one screen

WhatsApp has three jobs, in this order

Job 1 · days 0 to 7

Make the ritual happen

Tie each stick to something the customer already does: first chai, the first call, laptop closing, brushing at night. Reminders at the time they pick, not ours. "If this, then that" plans roughly double follow-through in studies.

Job 2 · days 3 to 30

Let them notice it themselves

We can't claim results, so we ask. Taste check, a one-tap 3 PM energy poll, their own sleep-tracker screenshot. When the customer says it, it is proof, and it is legal.

Job 3 · days 9 to 66

Make reordering zero effort

One-tap reorder before the box runs out, pause and skip without friction, subscribe-and-save, and a small reward for coming back after a miss. Keep going until day 66, when the habit should hold on its own.

Guardrails

WhatsApp rules, claims rules and our own limits

Never in a message
  • "Fixes your sleep", "cures insomnia", "treats anxiety", "boosts immunity"
  • "Recommended by doctors" or anything implying medical endorsement
  • "Take two for a bigger boost"
  • "Melatonin is dangerous, unlike us" or naming another brand
  • "Only 2 hours left!" when it isn't true, or "You broke your streak"
  • SILT improves X in Y days
Say this instead
  • "Part of your wind-down", "your morning stick", "before your first call"
  • "3 g glycine, the dose used in sleep studies"
  • "One stick a day, every dose printed on the box"
  • "How did this week feel?" and the customer's own words, with permission
  • "Your box has about 4 sticks left", plain and true
  • "Missed a day? Pick up tonight. Your streak waits."
