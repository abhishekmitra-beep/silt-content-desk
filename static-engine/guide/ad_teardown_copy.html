Rules for competitor inspiration

  • Keep the idea, never their words, layout, colours or logo
  • Never name a competitor in our ads
  • Only SILT's own facts, doses and prices (Rules tab)
  • No claim we can't stand behind, even if they make it
  • Our look: premium still life, real pack, no plinth, props or drink
  • Every word inside Meta's red lines, in every size
